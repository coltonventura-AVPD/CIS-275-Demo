Permits

Commuters

Green permit

$44

For students who don't live on campus. Park in green spaces during the day, but not overnight (1 AM to 6:30 AM).

Recommended

Blue permit

$88

The most common permit, open to any student. Park in blue spaces ("Blue Lagoon"), including overnight. Red spaces are allowed from 5 PM Friday to 6:30 AM Monday, but not during extended breaks.

Residence halls only

Grey permit

$175

Limited supply, closest to the halls. Grey permits park only in grey spaces, never in blue, green, or red.

Handicap, Red, and Visitor permits are covered in Section C of the parking manual.

Citations and appeals

An appeal has to argue that the citation was issued contrary to the written Parking Rules and Regulations. Arguments about the general intent of the rules, such as weekend closures, have been rejected. Appeals are handled administratively, not by the parking committee. If you think a citation was unfair, contact your Student Senate representative on the committee.

Commonly cited mistakes

  • Blue holders assume weekend red-space parking also covers longer breaks.
  • Grey holders park in blue spaces or use daytime parking.
  • Grey holders assume the blue-in-red weekend rule applies to them.
  • Students park on campus after buying a pass but before picking it up.
